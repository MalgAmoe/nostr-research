# Provisional vessel trial

Status: first same-field comparison completed on 2026-07-29.

## Purpose

Test whether two minimal caller-side postures produce meaningfully different
journeys from the same random evidence field after command-construction
friction has been reduced.

These are not engine concepts, configurations, classes, or permanent vessel
definitions. They are short instructions followed manually by the navigator.

## Shared protocol

- Acquire one bounded random kind-1 field.
- Reproduce that exact starting evidence in two independent process-local
  sessions.
- Use the same engine, controller, composer, relay defaults, and memory bounds.
- Make every operation and judgment explicit.
- Do not transfer evidence, handles, judgments, or acquired continuations
  between sessions.
- Count decisions made from receipts alone separately from decisions requiring
  summary, preview, details, coverage, inspect, or explain.
- Compare subjects encountered, movement sequence, senses used, judgment
  timing, collection, invalid commands, and resource pressure.

## Vessel A: Depth

Intent: turn one initially promising subject into one explainable anchor or an
explicit rejection.

1. Preview at most twelve starting notes and choose one without ranking the
   entire field.
2. Prefer depth: author/profile, authored evidence, then at most one
   relationship branch or second ring.
3. Use details or explain before treating an exact relationship as meaningful.
4. Judge only after profile and behavioral evidence have both been observed;
   uncertainty is a valid result.
5. Collect at most one anchor and its supporting evidence. Stop after two
   rings, 25 research commands, or 70% buffer pressure.

## Vessel B: Breadth

Intent: retain a varied, lightly qualified candidate set without descending
deeply into its first visible cluster.

1. Derive three visibly different signals from the starting field before
   choosing any candidate.
2. Give each signal at most one account/profile or evidence step; do not follow
   a second ring.
3. Prefer summaries and balanced previews for comparison; use details only for
   finalists.
4. Delay judgment until branches have been compared.
5. Collect up to five varied candidates with explicit qualifications. Stop at
   five candidates, 30 research commands, or 70% buffer pressure.

## Success criterion

The vessel premise gains support only if the two journeys diverge because of
these posture rules rather than different starting data or command friction.
Cosmetic differences, different prose, or merely different operation counts
are insufficient.

## Comparison

The journeys diverged for posture reasons:

| Dimension | Depth | Breadth |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Initial attention | One unusual note among twelve | Three field-wide signals |
| Movement | Note → author → history → references → second ring | Field → three branches → sampled authors → profiles |
| Senses | Repeated exact and sequential evidence | Comparative summaries and balanced previews |
| Judgment tempo | One judgment after behavioral and relational evidence | Three judgments delayed until branches were compared |
| Collection | One qualified subject plus 30 excerpts | Three varied candidates, no archived corpus |
| Added evidence | 50 events beyond the shared field | 8 profile events beyond the shared field |
| Lifecycle shape | 9 handles, 50% buffer pressure | 19 handles, 44% buffer pressure |

The difference was not command friction: both voyages had zero
construction-class failures and used the same composer selectively. It was not
starting data: both sessions began from the same canonical events and
observations.

This first comparison therefore supports the vessel premise at its smallest
useful scale. A short caller-side posture changed what was attended to, how the
navigator moved, which evidence became visible, when judgment occurred, and
what was carried out of the field.

It also exposes the tradeoff without turning it into a score:

- Depth produced substantially better explanation of one subject but was
  vulnerable to the topology of that subject's immediate relationships.
- Breadth resisted early gravitational capture and exposed field diversity,
  but its retained candidates remained intentionally shallow and its signals
  were more exposed to keyword spam.

No vessel framework or engine behavior is justified by one comparison. These
two posture cards are now usable experimental objects and may be revised only
after another voyage reveals concrete friction.

## Replication conclusion

Reversing voyage order did not collapse the difference:

| Stable pattern | Depth, first field | Depth, reversed field | Breadth, first field | Breadth, reversed field |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Collected subjects | 1 | 1 | 3 | 3 |
| Authored histories opened | 1 | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| Relationship/second-ring descent | yes | yes | no | no |
| Archived excerpts | 30 | 30 | 0 | 0 |
| Notebook judgments | 1 | 1 | 3 | 3 |
| Handles at exit | 9 | 7 | 19 | 19 |
| Buffer pressure | 50% | 49.7% | 44% | 44.1% |
| Construction failures | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

Buffer pressure is retained as an operational consequence of each voyage's
added evidence, not as a stable vessel fingerprint. The authored and
relationship descents versus profile-only hydration explain the more useful
underlying difference.

The subjects and final judgments changed with the field, as they should:
Depth produced one uncertain result and one qualified anchor; Breadth produced
different candidate categories. The movement, senses, judgment tempo,
collection shape, and lifecycle footprint remained recognizably vessel-shaped.

This replication is sufficient to treat Depth and Breadth as genuinely
distinct provisional vessels rather than one navigator's order effect. It does
not make either vessel universally better:

- Depth repeatedly achieved behavioral and relationship explanation at the
  cost of concentration on one topology.
- Breadth repeatedly preserved variety and exposed field composition at the
  cost of shallow evidence and repeated keyword-spam exposure.

The next question is no longer whether these two postures diverge. It is
whether either produces a useful artifact for its stated goal.

## Vessel C: Skeptic

Status: first expedition completed on 2026-07-29.

Intent: collect only promising profiles whose selection reason survives an
active attempt to invalidate it.

1. Begin from a fresh random field and identify up to three candidates that
   appear promising for explicit, possibly different reasons.
2. Record each initial reason and its uncertainty before descending.
3. For every candidate inspect profile evidence, authored behavior, and one
   route capable of weakening the initial reason—such as repetition,
   automation, contradictory activity, affiliation, or relationship context.
4. Keep the candidate unresolved until that evidence is observed. The final
   judgment may strengthen, weaken, reject, or preserve uncertainty.
5. Collect only candidates whose original reason survives in a qualified form,
   together with supporting and disconfirming evidence. Stop when three
   candidates survive, every candidate fails, 45 research commands are
   reached, or buffer pressure reaches 75%.

The vessel does not assume candidates are deceptive or bad. It changes the
order and discipline of judgment, not the conclusion.

Unlike Depth, its characteristic record is not one explained subject. For
every candidate it retains the initial reason, the attempted invalidation, and
the qualified outcome—even when that outcome is rejection or unresolved
uncertainty. Insufficient support does not automatically become negative
evidence.

### First expedition

A fresh acquisition produced 380 distinct kind-1 events. From 300 visible
authors, the navigator took a deterministic sample of 40 and resolved 13
profiles. Three candidates were chosen for deliberately different initial
reasons:

- `nwc-enclaved wallet service`: a concrete open-source NWC wallet claim
  involving a trusted execution environment;
- `Enprofmo`: claimed authorship of a podcast and a music track; and
- `KAZも`: an unusual Japanese personal identity describing flexibility and
  computer cleaning.

Each initial reason and uncertainty was recorded before authored evidence was
opened.

The wallet candidate failed active scrutiny. Its bounded 40-note authored
window consisted almost entirely of repeated operational statistics. A scan
found no release, source, enclave, attestation, or security discussion. This
does not refute the service's technical claims, but it invalidated the reason
for treating the account as a promising person to follow. It was marked
`uninterested` as a service bot.

The creator candidate remained unresolved. The relay returned one authored
note, and that note did support the podcast claim, but only as a promotional
introduction. One note could not establish an ongoing creative practice. The
candidate remained `uncertain` and was not collected; the vessel did not turn
missing evidence into a rejection.

The least conventionally impressive candidate survived. `KAZも`'s 40-note
window showed varied, situated personal activity: travel, food, games,
repairs, conversation, and an original image. The stream did not resemble
repeated automation and made the eccentric profile more coherent as a human
voice. The account was marked `interested`, added to the
`skeptic-survivors` membership, and preserved with its 40 evidence excerpts.

Measurements:

- Research commands: 33 before final synchronization, excluding
  configuration.
- Construction-class invalid commands: 1—a direct `show` requested a preview
  above the published maximum; the failure did not mutate the session.
- Handles at exit: 13.
- Observation buffer: 592 of 900 events, 65.8% pressure.
- Archive: 1 canonical profile and 40 excerpts.
- Notebook: 3 judgments and 1 survivor membership.
- Collection: 1 survivor from 3 initially promising candidates.
- Exit reason: all three candidates reached a qualified outcome before the
  command or pressure bounds.

### Skeptic conclusion

The posture changed more than movement. It changed which first impression was
allowed to become a collection:

- technical specificity did not survive behavioral evidence;
- a plausible creative claim remained unresolved rather than being promoted
  or unfairly rejected; and
- an obscure personal voice survived because varied authored behavior
  supported the original curiosity.

This is the first provisional vessel to make invalidation tempo central. Its
useful output was not merely a smaller candidate set: it retained the reason
each candidate entered, the evidence sought against that reason, and the
qualified outcome. The result supports judgment discipline as a genuine vessel
dimension while preserving the navigator as the sole owner of conclusions.

- 68 rows containing inquiry or first-person terms.

All three were contaminated in different ways. Media exposed price bots and

  and

- Research commands: 30 before final synchronization.
- Construction-class invalid commands: 0 in the clean replicated run.
- Handles at exit: 19.
- Observation buffer: 305 of 800 events, 38.1% pressure.
- Archive: empty.
- Notebook: 3 uncertain judgments and 1 membership.
- Collection: 3 qualified candidates.

### Hostile-field conclusion

The postures failed differently, which is more informative than asking which
one “won”:

- Depth escaped the obvious market bots but was captured by one coherent
  promotional topology. Its deeper senses exposed that capture clearly enough
  to reject the anchor.
- Breadth resisted single-cluster capture but could not make noisy signals
  clean. It retained variety by weakening its claims, not by eliminating the
  hostile field.

Neither vessel removes bullshit. In this field, Depth made one local failure
legible while Breadth made several kinds of contamination comparable. Their
observed effect was not filtering but a different arrangement of attention,
evidence, and qualification. A single tag and moment do not establish this as
a universal hostile-field result.

### Procedural finding

The three-profile collection was useful, but it was not produced within the
provisional Skeptic protocol. The voyage crossed both its 45-command and 75%
buffer-pressure stops while finding and investigating a replacement third
candidate. Final pressure was 85.1%, with 41 live handles.

This must not be repaired in prose after the fact. The result shows that the
current Skeptic card is adequate for testing a few already-visible candidates,
but not yet adequate for a cold-start collection goal that may require
directed reacquisition and candidate replacement.

The next question is therefore concrete: should a goal-oriented Skeptic voyage
use a smaller collection goal, a renewable field discipline, or a revised
resource envelope? That should be decided from another deliberately bounded
attempt, not by silently relaxing the existing vessel.

## Vessel D: Keyhole

Status: expedition protocol.

Intent: experience the field as pure locality—one exact subject and its
immediate relationships—and test whether an ordered trail of chosen doors can
be a useful collection.

Senses:

- Field summaries, aggregates, previews, scans, and field-level relations are
  forbidden.
- The starting field is acquired and position six is selected
  deterministically without preview.
- The visible world at any moment is the current exact subject, its evidenced
  immediate relationships, and its author's profile.
- Details and inspect expose the current room. Relationship collections may be
  summarized locally. Explain is required before an exact relationship is
  treated as meaningful.

Movement:

- Every forward step crosses one visible door: author, referenced event or
  account, ancestor, reply, quote, or mention.
- Local evidence is preferred. Relay continuation occurs only through an
  explicit navigator decision.
- Backtracking may return to any visited subject and choose another door.
- The starting field cannot be reacquired during the trail.

Suspected automation is a navigator conclusion supported by bounded evidence,
never an engine fact. If encountered, the navigator records the evidence and
may backtrack to a prior room. The encounter remains trail cargo.

Judgment follows ordinary navigator tempo. The vessel does not prescribe a
verdict.

Collection: an ordered caller-side trail recording each visited subject, the
door used to reach it, exact relationship evidence, and any navigator
judgment. Canonical preservation is optional and explicit.

Stop at eight visited subjects including the start, 35 research commands, 70%
buffer pressure, or recorded navigator abandonment.

Success requires every step to remain recoverable as an evidenced trail and
the resulting collection to differ in kind from an unordered profile or
membership set.

### First Keyhole voyage

Status: bounded voyage completed on 2026-07-29 with one sensory-protocol
violation.

The complete ordered trail is recorded in
[KEYHOLE-TRAIL.md](../artifacts/KEYHOLE-TRAIL.md). The blind sixth event led through:

```text
cryptic image note
→ its author
→ a loop back to the starting note
→ a second authored video reply
→ its exact reply parent
→ its exact thread root
→ the reply parent's author
```

Six distinct subjects were visited. Exact NIP-10 explanations preserved why
the parent and root doors were valid. The trail hit its 35-command bound at
41.9% buffer pressure, before reaching eight subjects.

The resulting ordered artifact is genuinely different from an unordered
profile collection. It preserves adjacency, backtracking, a loop, and the
reason each door was crossed. The sensory restriction produced a strong sense
of being somewhere rather than surveying a field.

However, the final room exposed a contradiction in the card. The navigator
suspected templated engagement and previewed five authored notes to obtain
repetition evidence. That collection preview violated Keyhole's senses. The
suspected-automation escape rule currently requires comparative evidence that
the vessel forbids or can obtain only by visiting notes individually at high
command cost.

The voyage therefore supports trail-as-collection and locality as a strong
sensory posture, but it does not pass the card unchanged. Before another
Keyhole flight, either remove the automation escape rule, permit one explicit
bounded diagnostic window, or require sequential visits and enlarge the
command envelope. This is a caller-side vessel decision, not an engine gap.

## Vessel E: Correspondent

Status: expedition protocol; boarding rule requires revision.

Intent: experience Nostr through dialogue rather than through a field, an
author feed, or topical aggregation.

Senses:

- The visible world after boarding is the current exact contribution, its
  evidenced place in a conversation, nearby contributions, and participating
  accounts.
- Details expose exact contributions. Explain is required before a reply,
  ancestor, quote, or mention is treated as a conversation edge.
- Field-wide topical, popularity, and author-frequency analysis is forbidden.

Movement:

- Traverse only replies, ancestors, quotes, mentions, and participants in the
  current conversation.
- An account is a participant, not an exit into its unrelated authored notes.
- Local evidence is preferred; relay continuation is an explicit navigator
  choice.
- Backtracking within the conversation is allowed.

Judgment follows ordinary navigator tempo. The vessel does not decide whether
a contribution is sincere, relevant, or human.

Collection: a bounded conversation map containing exact contributions,
participants, evidenced edges, and navigator judgments.

Stop at twelve contributions, 35 research commands, 70% buffer pressure, a
genuinely dry conversation, or recorded navigator abandonment.

### First Correspondent boarding experiment

Status: three bounded blind boardings completed on 2026-07-29; no conversation
voyage began.

The complete record is in
[CORRESPONDENT-VOYAGE.md](../artifacts/CORRESPONDENT-VOYAGE.md).

A 300-event random field was acquired but never previewed. Separate attempts
then selected fixed positions six, seven, and eight. Two positions contained
application-generated `zone_presence` data; the other was an untagged
recruitment or solicitation broadcast. Each bounded conversation query reached
EOSE on all three relays and returned only its seed.

This is not an engine failure. It demonstrates that a strict conversation
posture is viable only after boarding, while blind random-event selection is a
poor boarding convention for sparse conversations. Retrying invisibly until a
conversation appears would condition the random field without navigator
ownership.

The next Correspondent attempt should therefore use a small, explicit airlock:
show a bounded random set and mechanical conversation affordances, then let the
navigator choose a candidate or abandon the voyage. After that visible choice,
conversation-only movement remains strict.

## Vessel F: Echo Chamber

Status: expedition protocol.

Intent: perceive recurrence and concentration without converting those
measurements into an automatic social classification.

Senses:

- Exact repeated text, author concentration, recurring tags and domains, and
  repeated relationship shapes are foregrounded.
- Counts always retain their bounded field denominator and evidence lineage.
- Account metadata and exact events may be inspected to contextualize a
  recurrence.
- “Coordinated,” “automated,” “spam,” and “trustworthy” are never engine or
  vessel conclusions.

Movement:

- Begin with a random field and derive several independent recurrence views.
- Choose a visible recurrence and move to its carrier events and accounts.
- Compare concentrated single-author publication with distributed repeated
  content rather than collapsing both into one category.
- Any further acquisition is explicit and directed by the navigator.

Judgment belongs to the navigator. The vessel records measurements first and
keeps interpretations attributable.

Collection: a resonance map containing bounded signals, carrier identities,
exact evidence, and separately attributed navigator conclusions.

### First Echo Chamber voyage

Status: useful bounded analysis completed on 2026-07-29; final domain branch
ended in a transport timeout.

The full record is in
[ECHO-CHAMBER-VOYAGE.md](../artifacts/ECHO-CHAMBER-VOYAGE.md).

Within a random 500-event field, the vessel exposed two sharply different
structures:

- one account contributed 104 events, all with distinct text, occupying 20.8%
  of the field; and
- one exact solicitation appeared 75 times from 75 distinct accounts,
  occupying 15% of the field.

Hydration resolved metadata for the concentrated publisher, identifying the
current profile as `Web 4.0 Semantic Layer - aéPiot`. It resolved none of the
75 distributed identities in the bounded attempt. These remain mechanical
facts, not proof of automation or coordination.

The voyage demonstrates that resonance is a useful collection shape: it
distinguishes one loud publisher from many identities carrying one exact
message. It also exposed two operational cautions. Raw command guessing caused
fifteen avoidable, non-mutating errors despite the existing composer, and a
1,000-row domain explode followed by aggregation exceeded the controller's
30-second response timeout. The former is operator error. The latter warrants
controlled reproduction only if another voyage makes the same route important.

## Vessel G: Dowsing Rod

Status: expedition protocol.

Intent: expose low-frequency factual properties without treating rarity as
importance, quality, or uncertainty.

Senses:

- Cardinalities of formats, media families, domains, tags, authors, and
  relationship shapes are candidate senses.
- The navigator first observes whether a dimension meaningfully divides the
  bounded field.
- A rare pocket is inspected through exact evidence before it can become
  cargo.
- Rarity never supplies a notebook judgment.

Movement:

- Begin with a random field and compare several factual dimensions.
- Abandon dimensions that are uniform or primarily measure missing
  classification.
- Enter one bounded rare pocket chosen by the navigator.
- Hydrate or traverse only when the rare pocket itself makes that useful.

Collection: caller-chosen curiosities carrying the exact property that exposed
them and their supporting evidence.

## Vessel H: Lighthouse

## Vessel I: Archivist

Status: expedition protocol.

Intent: reconstruct an attributed context dossier around one selected subject
without claiming complete context, truth, or trust.

Senses:

- Exact focal evidence and relationship explanations are primary.
- Resolved, unresolved, boundedly absent, and deliberately unexpanded context
  remain distinct.
- Author metadata is an attributed current profile observation, not identity
  verification.
- The dossier always declares its expansion boundary.

Movement:

- Use a small visible airlock to choose a subject with contextual edges.
- Resolve every direct edge inside the declared dossier boundary.
- Perform bounded surrounding searches only when named in that boundary.
- Record second-hop and missing edges rather than recursively expanding them
  without limit.

Judgment remains with the navigator. The vessel does not decide whether a
claim is true, evidence is sufficient, or a source is credible.

Collection: a context dossier containing exact evidence, resolved direct
relationships, attributed metadata, bounded search outcomes, and explicit
unexpanded edges.
