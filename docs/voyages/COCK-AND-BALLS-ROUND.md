# Cock and Balls round

This deliberately anatomical composer tested whether temporary movement and
selected evidence benefit from different visible lifecycles.

## Instrument

- **Root:** one protected base handle.
- **Left and right balls:** independently named, bounded evidence reservoirs.
- **Tip:** the current handle.
- **Shaft:** the visible and retractable command path.
- **Thrust:** one ordinary controller command.
- **Pull:** retain the current tip in either ball.
- **Retract:** return to an earlier shaft point without erasing either ball.

The navigator owns the meaning of the two reservoirs. The composer only
retains handles and attributed reasons.

## Live voyage

The instrument rooted itself in a random 300-event field. The probe traversed
to event rows, aggregated event kinds, and exposed their distribution.

The navigator then:

1. retracted to the shared row-space;
2. filtered dominant kind 22668 and pulled its 107 rows into the left
   `gravity` reservoir;
3. retracted to the same row-space;
4. filtered unfamiliar kind 22575 and pulled its 3 rows into the right
   `anomaly` reservoir.

The shaft ended at the right-hand specimen while the left specimen survived
outside the active path. The root remained unchanged. Nine visible controller
commands completed the voyage.

## Surprise

The two reservoirs did not produce semantic opposition. Both contained
machine-signaling traffic: one common, one rare. Calling them `gravity` and
`anomaly` changed attention but did not make their contents different in kind.

This is not a mechanical failure. It distinguishes two questions:

- Did the instrument retain two explicitly different selections? **Yes.**
- Did those selections reveal meaningfully different regions? **No.**

The useful primitive is therefore not “two opposing balls.” It is two bounded
reservoirs whose relationship remains unresolved until the navigator examines
them. They may contain opposition, two scales of one phenomenon, complementary
evidence, or accidental redundancy.

The experiment should remain weird. Adding automatic diversity, polarity, or
classification would move judgment into the composer. Further voyages may
instead change what the navigator chooses to pull.

The reproducible live harness is `experiments/cock-and-balls-live.mjs`.
