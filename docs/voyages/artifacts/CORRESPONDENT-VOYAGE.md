# Correspondent voyage 1

Date: 2026-07-29

Status: bounded boarding experiment completed; no conversation voyage began.

## Posture

The Correspondent treats dialogue as the visible world. It may inspect exact
notes, traverse replies, ancestors, quotes, mentions, and participants, and
explain the evidence for those relationships. It may not escape through an
author's unrelated notes or use field-wide topical and popularity analysis.

The intended collection is a bounded conversation map: exact contributions,
participants, the evidenced relationship between contributions, and any
navigator judgments. The posture stops at twelve contributions, 35 research
commands, 70% buffer pressure, a genuinely dry conversation, or navigator
abandonment.

For this first experiment, boarding was deliberately blind. A 300-event random
kind-1 field was acquired from:

- `wss://nos.lol/`
- `wss://relay.primal.net/`
- `wss://relay.snort.social/`

The navigator did not preview or analyze the field. Three separate boarding
attempts used fixed positions six, seven, and eight. A failed attempt did not
silently advance to another candidate; each was recorded as a separate
outcome.

## Bounds and operational facts

- Initial field: 300 distinct events, bounded by the distinct-event budget.
- Boarding attempts: three, fixed in advance.
- Conversation results with more than one visible subject: zero.
- Final buffer: 302 of 800 events, 37.75% pressure.
- Final live handles: seven.
- Notebook and archive entries: zero.
- Protocol construction failures: zero.

The extra two resident events came from the bounded continuation acquisition
work, but they did not become evidenced conversation members in the returned
handles. The voyage therefore did not treat them as doors.

## Finding

Conversation-only movement is coherent once a conversation exists. Blind
selection from an undifferentiated random kind-1 field is not a coherent
boarding habit for it: three honest attempts produced three isolated seeds.

This is not evidence that Nostr lacks conversations, and it is not an engine
defect. The random field contained application traffic and unreferenced
broadcasts, while conversation is a sparse property from the perspective of a
random event.

The next Correspondent card should make boarding a visible navigator decision.
A small random-field airlock may expose a bounded set of candidates and their
mechanical conversation affordances. The navigator may then choose one or
abandon the voyage. The vessel must not automatically retry until it finds a
lively thread, because that would hide field conditioning. Once boarded, the
conversation-only restriction can remain strict.
