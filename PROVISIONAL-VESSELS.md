# Provisional vessel trial

Status: first same-field comparison completed on 2026-07-29.

## Purpose

Test whether two minimal caller-side postures produce meaningfully different
journeys from the same random evidence field after command-construction
friction has been reduced.

These are not engine concepts, configurations, classes, or permanent vessel
definitions. They are short instructions followed manually by the navigator.

## Shared protocol

- Acquire one bounded random kind-1 field.
- Reproduce that exact starting evidence in two independent process-local
  sessions.
- Use the same engine, controller, composer, relay defaults, and memory bounds.
- Make every operation and judgment explicit.
- Do not transfer evidence, handles, judgments, or acquired continuations
  between sessions.
- Count decisions made from receipts alone separately from decisions requiring
  summary, preview, details, coverage, inspect, or explain.
- Compare subjects encountered, movement sequence, senses used, judgment
  timing, collection, invalid commands, and resource pressure.

## Vessel A: Depth

Intent: turn one initially promising subject into one explainable anchor or an
explicit rejection.

1. Preview at most twelve starting notes and choose one without ranking the
   entire field.
2. Prefer depth: author/profile, authored evidence, then at most one
   relationship branch or second ring.
3. Use details or explain before treating an exact relationship as meaningful.
4. Judge only after profile and behavioral evidence have both been observed;
   uncertainty is a valid result.
5. Collect at most one anchor and its supporting evidence. Stop after two
   rings, 25 research commands, or 70% buffer pressure.

## Vessel B: Breadth

Intent: retain a varied, lightly qualified candidate set without descending
deeply into its first visible cluster.

1. Derive three visibly different signals from the starting field before
   choosing any candidate.
2. Give each signal at most one account/profile or evidence step; do not follow
   a second ring.
3. Prefer summaries and balanced previews for comparison; use details only for
   finalists.
4. Delay judgment until branches have been compared.
5. Collect up to five varied candidates with explicit qualifications. Stop at
   five candidates, 30 research commands, or 70% buffer pressure.

## Success criterion

The vessel premise gains support only if the two journeys diverge because of
these posture rules rather than different starting data or command friction.
Cosmetic differences, different prose, or merely different operation counts
are insufficient.

## Shared starting field

One bounded relay acquisition produced exactly 300 distinct kind-1 events and
353 accepted observations. The canonical events and their relay observations
were copied into two independent 700-event process-local memories before
either voyage began.

Both sessions selected the same 300-event newest-first field. Subsequent relay
contact, handles, judgments, and memory changes remained isolated.

## Depth result

The first twelve notes contained machine traffic, news automation, commercial
posts, and one unusual first-person note describing a “tornado in space.” The
navigator chose that sixth note without ranking the full field.

Exact details showed a signed kind-1 event containing inline Nostr references.
The author resolved as `rawan`, with the minimal profile description
`magicienne`. A 30-note authored window revealed a coherent but highly
performative stream about moving planets, being a phenomenon, humor, work, and
repeated inline references.

One relationship branch produced three referenced accounts. Exact membership
explanation showed both tagged mentions and inline account references from
specific authored events. Hydrating that second ring resolved `paulinthejungle`
(“Building Primal”), `jack`, and `miljan`.

The starting account was recorded as `uncertain` at strength `0.4`, not as an
anchor. The authored voice was coherent, but the evidence was insufficient to
interpret the persona confidently and the relationship branch pulled directly
toward highly central Primal identities. Thirty authored excerpts were
preserved.

Measurements:

- Composed commands: 3 (`continue`, `remember`, `preserve`).
- Construction-class invalid commands: 0.
- Handles at exit: 9.
- Observation buffer: 350 of 700 events, 50% pressure.
- Archive: 30 excerpts.
- Notebook: 1 uncertain judgment.
- Research decisions made from receipts alone: 0.
- Evidence-requiring decisions: 5—seed selection, authored descent,
  relationship-branch selection, second-ring hydration, and final judgment.
- Habitual senses: starting preview, exact note details, profile preview,
  authored preview, relationship summary, relationship details, exact
  membership explanation, and second-ring profile preview.
- Exit reason: two rings completed with an explainable uncertain result.

## Breadth result

The navigator related the same 300 starting events and established three
signals before selecting any account:

- 41 media-bearing rows;
- 17 creative-term matches; and
- 8 inquiry-term matches.

The creative and inquiry previews immediately exposed heavy semantic-search
automation and keyword spam. That did not cause a deep spam investigation; the
posture retained the branch fact and continued comparison.

Each signal supplied a deterministic three-account sample. Their union resolved
six profiles:

- `Christian Lacdael`;
- `captjack`;
- `The Meme Bay`;
- `Netasgard News`;
- `Web 4.0 Semantic Layer - aéPiot`; and
- `Brasidas`.

After one combined profile comparison, three varied finalists were inspected
in detail and retained:

- Christian Lacdael, qualified as a creative candidate whose poetry/programming
  identity remained unverified by authored history;
- The Meme Bay, qualified as a persistent media publisher without claims about
  originality or quality; and
- Netasgard News, qualified as a French-language linked-source news feed
  without deeper verification.

All three judgments remained `uncertain`. The semantic-search account and two
less informative profiles were not collected. No authored history or second
ring was opened.

Measurements:

- Research commands: 29, excluding configuration and final synchronization.
- Composed commands: 3 (one filter and two scans).
- Construction-class invalid commands: 0.
- Handles at exit: 19.
- Observation buffer: 308 of 700 events, 44% pressure.
- Archive: empty.
- Notebook: 3 uncertain judgments.
- Research decisions made from receipts alone: 0.
- Evidence-requiring decisions: 5—branch comparison, finalist selection, and
  three individual qualifications.
- Habitual senses: one signal summary, two balanced signal previews, one
  combined profile preview, and finalist details.
- Exit reason: three varied candidates were qualified within the
  thirty-command limit; five responsible candidates were not required.

## Comparison

The journeys diverged for posture reasons:

| Dimension | Depth | Breadth |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Initial attention | One unusual note among twelve | Three field-wide signals |
| Movement | Note → author → history → references → second ring | Field → three branches → sampled authors → profiles |
| Senses | Repeated exact and sequential evidence | Comparative summaries and balanced previews |
| Judgment tempo | One judgment after behavioral and relational evidence | Three judgments delayed until branches were compared |
| Collection | One qualified subject plus 30 excerpts | Three varied candidates, no archived corpus |
| Added evidence | 50 events beyond the shared field | 8 profile events beyond the shared field |
| Lifecycle shape | 9 handles, 50% buffer pressure | 19 handles, 44% buffer pressure |

The difference was not command friction: both voyages had zero
construction-class failures and used the same composer selectively. It was not
starting data: both sessions began from the same canonical events and
observations.

This first comparison therefore supports the vessel premise at its smallest
useful scale. A short caller-side posture changed what was attended to, how the
navigator moved, which evidence became visible, when judgment occurred, and
what was carried out of the field.

It also exposes the tradeoff without turning it into a score:

- Depth produced substantially better explanation of one subject but was
  vulnerable to the topology of that subject's immediate relationships.
- Breadth resisted early gravitational capture and exposed field diversity,
  but its retained candidates remained intentionally shallow and its signals
  were more exposed to keyword spam.

No vessel framework or engine behavior is justified by one comparison. These
two posture cards are now usable experimental objects and may be revised only
after another voyage reveals concrete friction.
