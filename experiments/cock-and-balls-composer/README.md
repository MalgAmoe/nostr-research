# Cock and Balls composer

A disposable anatomical research instrument above the neutral controller.

- **Root** is the protected base.
- **Left and right balls** are independently named, bounded evidence
  reservoirs.
- **Tip** is the current handle.
- **Shaft** is the visible, retractable path of ordinary commands.
- **Thrust** executes exactly one ordinary controller command.
- **Pull** retains the current tip in either reservoir.
- **Retract** moves the tip back without erasing collected evidence.

The anatomy is operational rather than decorative: temporary movement and
selected evidence have visibly different lifecycles.
